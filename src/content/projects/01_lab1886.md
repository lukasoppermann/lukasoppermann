##### From ideas to shipped products

At Daimler's incubator, I split my time between hands-on product design and building the conditions for the team to do good work. Alongside projects, I hired and mentored designers and defined the team's tools and processes.

##### Making design a shared practice

I introduced show-and-tell sessions for critique across projects and a presentation format to make design more visible within the company. Involving stakeholders early and improving tooling with other disciplines made collaboration part of the process, rather than a handoff at the end.
