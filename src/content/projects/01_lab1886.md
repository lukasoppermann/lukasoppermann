##### Getting design involved earlier

I worked with leadership to give design a stronger voice in product decisions. We started challenging business-driven assumptions and bringing the user perspective into early discussions and workshops, helping teams focus on solving real problems rather than just executing ideas.

##### Working better together

I introduced early design reviews with engineers so we could discuss ideas, identify problems and find solutions together. Their technical knowledge and platform expertise helped shape our designs. For example, I introduced dedicated Android designs instead of simply adapting the iOS version.

I also hired and mentored designers, introduced cross-project design critiques and established shared practices to improve how the team worked together.
